## If a session happens

Thirty minutes, one person at a time. Write down their words.

1. When did you last comment, and what were you trying to make happen?
2. Show a comment you would still send. What makes it yours?
3. Show a comment you would not want under your name. What failed?
4. Which claims are allowed in public? Which numbers are off limits?
5. When would you rather say nothing?
6. If the tool remembered one correction, what would it be?
7. Where does automation become uncomfortable?

Then bring five posts they recognize. Ask skip or comment before showing a draft. Count disagreements.
